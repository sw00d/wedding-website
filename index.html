Travel Details

Getting There and Back

We strongly recommend getting Global Entry before the wedding. It gives you access to expedited SENTRI lanes at the land border, which makes a significant difference whether you're walking or driving across.

Airports

For guests traveling by air, there are two convenient airport options for getting to Rosarito.

San Diego International Airport (SAN) 🇺🇸

San Diego is our recommended airport for most guests, offering plenty of domestic flight options and often more affordable fares. From the airport, you can take an Uber or Lyft to the San Ysidro border crossing (approximately 20–30 minutes), walk across into Mexico, and request an Uber to your accommodations in Rosarito.

On Friday, we recommend landing by 12pm to accommodate border crossing time prior to the welcome event.

On Sunday, we recommend getting a flight with a departure later than 3pm to accommodate border crossing time.

Tijuana International Airport (TIJ) 🇲🇽

Tijuana is the closest airport on the Mexican side of the border. Flying directly into Tijuana allows you to skip the land border crossing on arrival and take an Uber or taxi straight to your accommodations in Rosarito.

We recommend comparing flights into both airports to find the option that works best for your travel plans!

Getting to Rosarito

For guests flying into San Diego, we recommend crossing into Mexico on foot through the San Ysidro Port of Entry and taking an Uber to your accommodations in Rosarito. This is often an affordable and convenient alternative to flying directly into Tijuana.

Border Crossing: 720 E. San Ysidro Blvd., San Ysidro, CA 92173

Before You Cross: Guests entering Mexico on foot will need to complete a Forma Migratoria Múltiple (FMM), also known as a Mexican Tourist Card. We recommend completing the form online before your trip and bringing a printed copy along with your passport.

Once you've crossed into Tijuana, you can request an Uber to your lodging. The drive takes approximately 40–50 minutes, depending on traffic.

Returning to San Diego

For your return trip, we recommend arranging a taxi from your accommodation to the San Ysidro pedestrian border crossing, then walking across into the United States. Ubers are not reliably available for return trips from Rosarito to the border. We've provided a trusted taxi contact on our website to help you arrange your return transportation.

Once across the border, you can take an Uber or Lyft to San Diego International Airport, approximately 20–30 minutes away without heavy traffic.

Border Wait Times

With Global Entry: Global Entry members can use the expedited SENTRI pedestrian lanes, where waits are often around 10–30 minutes.

Without Global Entry: Standard pedestrian crossings can take approximately 30 minutes to 4 hours, with longer waits possible during busy weekends.

Traveling together? If one person has Global Entry and the other doesn't, you'll need to cross separately or use the standard pedestrian lanes together.

We recommend allowing plenty of extra time before your departing flight, especially on Sundays. You can check current border wait times through U.S. Customs and Border Protection.

Important: Please bring a valid passport book for your trip to Mexico and return to the United States. Global Entry members should also bring their physical Global Entry cards to access expedited pedestrian lanes.
